# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public marketing and discovery pages
- Public content pages that can be shared or indexed
- Public directories and profile pages reachable without login

## Out of scope
- Authenticated dashboard and member-only routes
- Admin pages under `/admin/**`
- Protected routes behind login gates
- Native mobile-only flows under `/mobile/**` except where they affect public share URLs

## Target audience
- Voters, volunteers, candidates, and people researching anti-corruption politics

## Primary keywords
- anti-corruption party
- political candidates
- political parties
- political lobbying
- democracy platform
